# Behavioral Profile

Based on career interview examples in `01-candidate-profile.md`, not a formal behavioral assessment. Preferences not discussed remain unknown.

## Strongest Behaviors
- **Ownership and initiative:** Investigates product needs and delivers through design, integration and rollout; examples include merchant payments and company tagging.
- **Quality:** Uses incremental tests and TDD; introduced testing pipelines and a test-review initiative. Reported positive feedback is not an audited defect metric.
- **Collaboration:** Coordinates with product, legal, fintech, mobile and analytics teams; shares technical knowledge and occasionally supports other developers.
- **Business context:** Clarifies needs and feasibility with stakeholders, including product and legal discussions around advertising balances.

## How You Work Best
- Interested in technically challenging backend work and growing product responsibility; ordinary backend tasks are also acceptable.
- Remote work is required; optional office access is acceptable and rare visits may be discussed. See the candidate profile for exact conditions.
- Broader environment and decision-making preferences have not been established.

## Growth Areas
- Interested in building more AI and agent-engineering experience. Course completion and internal AI tooling work do not establish production agent development expertise.
- No regular mentoring or formal people management has been established; describe the specific support and interviewing experience instead.

## Mapping to Job Posting Language

- Do not infer a culture mismatch or personal aversion to maintenance without evidence; ask about the actual work and team.

## Management Style Preferences
Not discussed; ask rather than infer.
